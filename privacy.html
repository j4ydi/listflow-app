Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026.

ListFlow requests one Google permission, drive.file, which only covers files that the app itself created or opened. It cannot see other files in Google Drive.

Those files stay in the signed-in user's Google Drive and on that user's computer. ListFlow does not sell Google user data or share it with third parties, and does not use it for advertising.

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account page under third-party connections.

Questions can be sent through this project's GitHub page.
